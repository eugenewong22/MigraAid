Your pay slip must be given with your payment. If it cannot be given then, it must be given within three working days of payment. If your job ends through termination or dismissal, the pay slip must be given together with your outstanding salary.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> - Give together with payment to employee.
> - If unable to give together, to be given within three working days of payment.
> - In the case of termination or dismissal, must give pay slip together with outstanding salary.
