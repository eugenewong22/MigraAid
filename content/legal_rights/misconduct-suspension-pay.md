title: Can my employer suspend me during a misconduct inquiry?

If an employer suspends an employee during an inquiry, the suspension cannot be more than 1 week without the Commissioner for Labour’s approval. The employee must be paid at least half their salary during suspension.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> You must not suspend your employee for more than 1 week without the Commissioner for Labour’s approval.
> - You must pay your employee at least half their salary during suspension.
