You or your employer can end the contract before the notice period ends by paying salary in lieu of notice. This is money equal to the salary you would have earned during the required notice period.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Either you or your employer may terminate a contract without waiting for the notice period to end.
> You can do so by paying the other party salary in lieu of notice . This is money equivalent to the salary that you would have earned during the required notice period.
