A resignation or termination notice must be in writing. It can be a termination letter or email. If your employer ends your job and you did not receive a termination letter, you can ask the employer to give you one.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Any notice of termination, either by you or your employer, must be in writing, such as through a termination letter or email.
> If you did not receive a termination letter, ask your employer to give you one.
