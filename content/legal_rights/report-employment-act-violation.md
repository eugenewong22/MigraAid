If you come across an employer who does not follow the rules, you can report an Employment Act violation, call MOM at 6438 5122, or submit feedback or an enquiry. MOM will keep your identity strictly confidential.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If you come across any non-compliant employers, you can:
> 
> - Report an Employment Act violation
> - Call MOM at 6438 5122 .
> - Submit feedback or enquiry Your identity will be kept strictly confidential.
