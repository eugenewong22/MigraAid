Your employer cannot reject your resignation. You can resign at any time if you serve the required notice or pay salary in lieu of notice. This means money equal to the salary you would have earned during the required notice period.

> Your employer cannot reject your resignation. You have the right to resign at any time, by serving the required notice or by paying salary in lieu of notice.
