If your employer wants to end your job because of misconduct, they must hold an inquiry first before taking disciplinary action. An inquiry is a process to look into what happened.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If termination is due to misconduct , your employer must hold an inquiry before taking any disciplinary action.
