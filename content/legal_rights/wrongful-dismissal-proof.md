If you were dismissed without notice, your employer must show that the dismissal was not wrongful. If you were dismissed with notice or salary instead of notice, you must show that it was wrongful.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> For dismissals without notice, your employer must show proof that the dismissal was not wrongful.
> For dismissals with notice or salary in lieu of notice, you must show proof that your dismissal was wrongful.
