If you are a local or foreign employee under a contract of service or apprenticeship, WICA covers you. Your salary, age and citizenship do not change this.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> The Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA) covers any local or foreign employee who is under a contract of service or contract of apprenticeship, regardless of salary, age or citizenship.
