If there is an objection to the permanent incapacity assessment, a Medical Board panel will assess your injuries again. The panel has two senior consultants from restructured hospitals. The person who objects must pay the Medical Board fee and any test costs required by the board.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> - Your injuries will be re-assessed by a panel appointed by the Work Injury Compensation Medical Board. The panel will comprise two senior consultants from restructured hospitals.
> - The objecting party will have to pay the Medical Board fee and the costs of any tests required by the board.
