---
title: Can my employer keep my passport?
source_ref: MOM — Passport retention advisory
---
Your passport belongs to you. In Singapore, employers and employment agents should not hold your
passport or work permit against your will. The Ministry of Manpower (MOM) advises that your personal
documents, including your passport, should stay in your possession.

If your employer has taken your passport and refuses to return it, you do not have to accept this.
You can seek help from MOM or from a migrant worker organisation such as HOME or TWC2, who can
assist you in getting your documents back.
