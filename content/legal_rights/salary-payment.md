---
title: When must my salary be paid?
source_ref: Employment Act — salary payment
---
Under Singapore's Employment Act, your salary must be paid at least once a month. It must be paid
within 7 days after the end of each salary period. Payment for overtime work must be made within
14 days after the salary period.

Your employer must give you an itemised pay slip showing your salary and any deductions. If your
salary is late, short, or unpaid, this is a salary dispute. You can raise it with the Tripartite
Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM), and a migrant worker organisation can help you file it.
