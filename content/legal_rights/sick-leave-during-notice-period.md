If you are covered by the Employment Act, paid or unpaid sick leave during your notice period counts as part of the notice period. Your employer cannot extend the notice period or claim short notice from you because of this sick leave.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If you are covered by the Employment Act , and you take any paid or unpaid sick leave during the notice period, it is treated as part of the notice period. Your employer cannot extend your notice period or claim any short notice from you.
