If your employment contract does not state a notice period, the notice you need to give depends on how long you worked. Less than 26 weeks: 1 day. 26 weeks to less than 2 years: 1 week. 2 years to less than 5 years: 2 weeks. 5 years or more: 4 weeks.

> If your employment contract didn’t specify the notice period, the notice period required will depend on your length of service.
