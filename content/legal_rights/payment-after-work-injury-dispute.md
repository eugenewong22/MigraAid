After a work injury dispute is resolved, MOM will issue a Certificate of Order to you, your employer and the insurer. They have 21 days to pay the compensation.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Once the dispute is resolved, MOM will issue a Certificate of Order to all parties: you (employee), employer and insurer. They have 21 days to pay the compensation.
