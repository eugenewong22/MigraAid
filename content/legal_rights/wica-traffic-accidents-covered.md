WICA covers a traffic accident when you are travelling between home and work in company transport that is not public transport. It also covers an accident when you are travelling for work, as long as you did not make a personal detour.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> - Meet with a traffic accident while taking company transport between home and workplace. The vehicle is not a public transport.
> - Meet with a traffic accident when travelling during work and for work purpose (e.g. travel from workplace to venue for meeting), regardless of the mode of transport. You did not make any personal detour.
