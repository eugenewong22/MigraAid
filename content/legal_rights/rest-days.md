---
title: Am I entitled to a rest day?
source_ref: Employment Act — rest days
---
If you are covered by the Employment Act, you are entitled to at least one rest day each week. A
rest day is a full continuous period of 24 hours, and it is unpaid.

Your employer decides which day is your rest day and should let you know in advance. If your
employer asks you to work on your rest day, you are entitled to extra pay for that day.
