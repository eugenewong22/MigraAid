If you are a manager or executive and were dismissed with notice or salary instead of notice, you can file a wrongful dismissal claim only if you served your employer for at least 6 months.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> For managers and executives who were dismissed with notice or salary in lieu of notice, you can only file a wrongful dismissal claim if you have served your employer for at least 6 months .
