For overtime work, your employer must pay at least 1.5 times your hourly basic rate of pay. The payment must be made within 14 days after the last day of the salary period.

> For overtime work, your employer must pay you at least 1.5 times the hourly basic rate of pay. Payment must be made within 14 days after the last day of the salary period.
