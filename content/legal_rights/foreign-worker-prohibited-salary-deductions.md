Your employer cannot deduct your salary for keeping your job or for employment costs. This includes work pass renewal, security bond, medical insurance, repatriation costs, compulsory training, medical fees and levy payment.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Your employer is not allowed to make deductions to your salaries under any circumstances, for the following purposes as specified in the Employment of Foreign Manpower Act:
> 
> - As a condition for employing or continuing to employ you.
> - For costs related to your employment:
> - Work pass renewal
> - Security bond
> - Medical insurance
> - Repatriation costs
> - Compulsory training
> - Medical fees
> - Levy payment
