If you are covered by the Employment Act, have worked for your employer for at least 3 months, and informed or tried to inform your employer within 48 hours of being absent, you can get paid outpatient sick leave and paid hospitalisation leave.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> You are entitled to paid outpatient sick leave and paid hospitalisation leave if:
> 
> - You are covered under the Employment Act.
> - You have served your employer for at least 3 months.
> - You have informed or tried to inform your employer within 48 hours of your absence.
