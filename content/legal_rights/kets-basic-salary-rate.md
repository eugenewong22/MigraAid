Your KETs should state your basic salary. If you are paid by the hour, day or piece of work, they should also state your basic pay rate for each hour, day or piece.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> 8
> Basic salary.
> For hourly, daily or piece-rated workers, employers should also indicate the basic rate of pay (e.g. $X per hour, day or piece).
