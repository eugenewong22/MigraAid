If you entered a contract of service on or after 1 April 2016, are covered by the Employment Act, and your contract is for 14 days or more, your employer must give you written key employment terms (KETs). The 14 days means the length of the contract, not the number of days you work.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Employers must issue KETs in writing to all employees who meet all the following requirements:
> 
> - Enter into a contract of service on or after 1 April 2016.
> - Are covered by the Employment Act.
> - Are employed for 14 days or more.
