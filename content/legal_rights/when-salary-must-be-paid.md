If you are covered by the Employment Act, your employer must pay your salary at least once a month. They may pay more often. Your salary must be paid within 7 days after the salary period ends. Overtime pay must be paid within 14 days after the salary period ends.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If you are covered by the Employment Act , your employer must pay your salary at least once a month.
> They can also pay it at shorter intervals if they choose.
> Salary must be paid:
> 
> - Within 7 days after the end of the salary period
> - For overtime work , within 14 days after the end of the salary period
