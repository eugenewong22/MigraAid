If you were wrongfully dismissed, including during pregnancy, a claim can ask for reinstatement or compensation. Reinstatement means returning to your job. If you are a manager or executive dismissed with notice or salary instead of notice, or you resigned involuntarily, you must have worked for the employer for at least 6 months before filing this claim.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If you have been wrongfully dismissed from service (including during pregnancy), you may file a claim for:
> 
> - Reinstatement
> - Compensation If you are a manager or executive who has been dismissed with notice or salary in-lieu of notice according to your terms of employment, or have resigned involuntarily, you must have served your employer for at least 6 months to submit a claim for wrongful dismissal.
