If you are a migrant worker employed on a Work Permit, your employer must buy and keep medical insurance for you. Your employer cannot make you pay the insurance cost.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> You must buy and maintain medical insurance (MI) for each migrant worker you employ. The insurance cost cannot be passed on to your workers.
