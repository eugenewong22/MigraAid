The medical examination checks for infectious diseases and whether you are fit to work. It screens for tuberculosis, HIV, syphilis and malaria.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> The medical examination screens the worker for 4 types of infectious diseases (tuberculosis, HIV, syphilis and malaria) and checks if they are fit to work.
