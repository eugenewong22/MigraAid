For the 6ME, the examination must be done by a doctor registered in Singapore. Your employer must pay for the 6ME and medical costs that arise from it.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> The 6ME must be conducted by a Singapore-registered doctor. As the employer, you must pay for the 6ME and any medical expenses arising from it..
