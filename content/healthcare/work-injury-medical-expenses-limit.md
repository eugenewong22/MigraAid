For a work accident, your employer must pay related medical expenses up to the maximum limit or 1 year from the accident date, whichever comes first.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Your employer will be required to pay for medical expenses related to a work accident up to the maximum limit or 1 year from the date of the accident, whichever comes first.
