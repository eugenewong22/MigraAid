When are children vaccinated? To prevent diseases such as measles and mumps, babies are vaccinated under the Childhood Immunisation Schedule from birth to 12-years-old.

> To prevent diseases such as measles and mumps, babies are vaccinated according to the Childhood Immunisation Schedule from birth to 12-years-old.
