If you hold a Work Permit, your employer's medical insurance must cover inpatient care and day surgery. It must also cover hospital bills for illnesses or conditions that may not be related to work. The coverage must be at least $60,000 per year.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> As an employer, you must buy and maintain MI with coverage for inpatient care and day surgery, including hospital bills for conditions that may not be work-related. The coverage for each Work Permit holder has to be at least $60,000 per year .
