Can HealthHub ask for money or bank details by phone? No. HealthHub will not ask you to transfer money or give bank log-in details in a phone call. If you are unsure, you can call the 24/7 ScamShield Helpline at 1799 or visit the ScamShield website.

> HealthHub will never ask you to transfer money or disclose bank log-in details over a phone call. Call the 24/7 ScamShield Helpline (1799) or visit ScamShield website if in doubt.
