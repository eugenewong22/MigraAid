If your 6ME is due while you are on overseas leave, your employer can request to postpone it until you return. For a postponement of more than 4 weeks, documents explaining the reason are needed.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If your worker is due for her 6ME while she is away on overseas leave , you can request to postpone it until she returns.
> If you need to postpone the 6ME by more than 4 weeks, you will need to upload documents to tell us why.
