If you have worked for at least 3 months, your employer must pay your medical consultation fee when it leads to at least one day of paid sick leave. The medical certificate must be from a doctor at a public medical institution or a doctor appointed by your company.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> In accordance with the Employment Act, if you have worked for at least 3 months:
> 
> - Your employer must pay for your medical consultation fee if:
> - it results in at least one day of paid sick leave, and
> - it arises from a medical certificate given to you by a medical practitioner from a public medical institution or appointed by your company.
