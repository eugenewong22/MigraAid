---
title: Does my employer have to pay for my medical care?
source_ref: MOM — Employer medical obligations
---
Employers of migrant workers in Singapore are responsible for their workers' medical care.
Employers must buy medical insurance for their Work Permit and S Pass holders, and must bear the
cost of treatment for work-related injuries.

If you are injured at work, report it to your employer and seek treatment. You should not be made
to pay for necessary treatment for a work injury yourself. If your employer refuses to help with a
work injury, you can contact MOM or a migrant worker organisation such as HOME or HealthServe.
