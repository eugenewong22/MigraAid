---
title: When must my Work Permit be cancelled after I leave Singapore?
status: published
source_id: mom-wp-cancel
source_ref: MOM — Cancel a Work Permit
source_url: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/cancel-a-work-permit
source_retrieved_at: 2026-07-26
---
If you have left Singapore and will not return to work for the employer, the employer must cancel your Work Permit within 1 week from your departure date.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If your worker has left Singapore and will not return to work for you, you must cancel the Work Permit within 1 week from the departure date .
