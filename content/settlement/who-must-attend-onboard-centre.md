You must attend the Onboard programme if you are a male non-Malaysian Work Permit holder in the Construction, Marine shipyard or Process sector, and you hold an in-principle approval (IPA).

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Male non-Malaysian Work Permit holders in the Construction, Marine shipyard and Process (CMP) sectors holding an in-principle approval (IPA) must attend the Onboard programme at the Onboard centre.
