If you and your employer cannot agree on new terms, either of you can end the employment with notice. Either side can also refer the disagreement to the Commissioner for Labour.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> If you cannot agree to new terms, both of you have the right to terminate employment with notice.
> Either party can also refer disagreements to the Commissioner for Labour
