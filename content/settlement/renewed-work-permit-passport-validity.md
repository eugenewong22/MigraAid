A renewed Work Permit is usually valid for 2 years. If the worker's passport expires in less than 2 years, the renewed permit will end 1 month before the passport expiry date.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Duration of renewal
> The Work Permit is usually valid for 2 years . However, it may be shorter depending on:
> 
> - The expiry of the worker’s passport. If it expires in less than 2 years, the duration will be until 1 month before the expiry date.
