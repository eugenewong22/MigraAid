Before checking in to the Onboard centre, bring your full IPA letter, your physical passport, and vaccination proof if you have it. You also need a working smartphone to download essential mobile apps.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Ensure that your worker carries with him the following before he checks in to the Onboard centre:
> 
> - Physical or digital copy of the worker's full set of IPA letter .
> - Physical passport.
> - Physical or digital documentary proof of vaccination (if any).
> - A working smartphone to download essential mobile applications.
