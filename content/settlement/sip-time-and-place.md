If you work in manufacturing, or you are a female CMP worker, attend SIP within 2 weeks after arriving in Singapore. The location is MWC Recreation Club @ Soon Lee. If you are a male CMP worker, attend at the Onboard centre when you arrive.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> When to attend
> Workers in the manufacturing sector and female CMP workers: Within 2 weeks of arrival in Singapore
> Male CMP workers: At the Onboard centre upon arrival
> 
> Where to attend
> Workers in the manufacturing sector and female CMP workers: MWC Recreation Club @ Soon Lee
> Male CMP workers: Onboard centre
