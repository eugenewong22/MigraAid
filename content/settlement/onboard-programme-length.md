If this is your first time working in Singapore, or you return after two years from the cancellation date of your last work pass, the Onboard programme is 3 days. If you return within two years of that cancellation date, it is 2 days.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> - First time working in Singapore or returning after two years from cancellation date of his last work pass: 3-day Onboard programme
> - Returning within two years of the cancellation date of his last work pass: 2-day Onboard programme
