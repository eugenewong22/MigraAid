After the Work Permit is issued, the notification letter is valid for 1 month from its issue date. It lets the worker start work and travel in and out of Singapore while waiting for the Work Permit card.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> The notification letter is valid for 1 month from the date of issue and:
> - Allows the worker to start work and travel in and out of Singapore while waiting for the Work Permit card.
