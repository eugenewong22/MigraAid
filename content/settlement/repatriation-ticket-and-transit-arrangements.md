Before buying your air ticket, the employer must discuss and agree with you in writing about transit arrangements and money needed during transit. The employer must buy a one-way ticket with a departure date within 14 days from Work Permit cancellation.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> - D iscuss and agree in writing with your worker on the following before buying an air ticket:
> - Transit arrangements, such as connecting flights and layover hours
> - The allowance needed to sustain your worker during the transit
> - Buy a one-way travel ticket with a departure date within 14 days from the Work Permit cancellation.
