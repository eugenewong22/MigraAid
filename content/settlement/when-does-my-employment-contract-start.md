A contract starts when the new worker turns up for work on the agreed starting date. If the worker does not turn up, the Employment Claims Act does not apply because the worker has not started work.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> The contract is in effect when the new recruit turns up for work on the appointed starting date.
> If the recruit fails to turn up:
> 
> - The Employment Claims Act does not apply, as the employee has not started work.
