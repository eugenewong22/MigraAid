You must attend SIP if you are a non-Malaysian Work Permit holder working in manufacturing for the first time in Singapore. You must also attend if you work in Construction, Marine shipyard or Process (CMP) for the first time in Singapore, or if you have an in-principle approval (IPA) and return more than two years after your last work pass was cancelled.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> All non-Malaysian Work Permit holders who are:
> 
> - Working in the manufacturing sector: First time working in Singapore
> - Working in the Construction, Marine shipyard and Process (CMP) sectors: First time working in Singapore or workers holding an in-principle approval (IPA) returning to work in Singapore more than two years after the cancellation date of their last work pass
