---
title: What should I know about my Work Permit?
source_ref: MOM — Work Permit conditions
---
Your Work Permit shows that you are allowed to work in Singapore for a specific employer. Keep your
permit and identity documents in your own possession.

You may work only for the employer stated on your permit, and only in the job it allows. If your
employment ends or your details change, your employer must inform MOM. If you have questions about
your permit status, contact MOM directly rather than relying on informal advice from agents or
other workers.
