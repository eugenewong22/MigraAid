Calculate overtime pay for a monthly-rated employee

If you are a monthly-rated employee covered under Part 4 of the Employment Act, use this calculator to find out your pay for working overtime.

You can claim overtime if you are:

- A non-workman earning a monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less.
- A workman earning a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less. Generally, a workman is someone whose work involves mainly manual labour. Find out if you fall under this category .

Overtime work is all work in excess of your normal hours of work (excluding breaks).
