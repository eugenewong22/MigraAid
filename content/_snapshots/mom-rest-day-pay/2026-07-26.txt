Calculate pay for work on a rest day

If you are covered by the Employment Act , use this calculator to calculate your pay for working on a rest day. A rest day comprises 1 whole day (midnight to midnight). It is not a paid day.

You can claim pay for work on a rest day if you are:

- A non-workman earning a monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less.
- A workman earning a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less.
