Your employer must provide housing that meets MOM requirements. They must pay the rent on time. If the housing contract is ending soon, they must renew it quickly or find other housing for you.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> - Provide your migrant workers with proper housing that meets our requirements .
> - Pay their rent on time.
> - If their housing contract is expiring soon, promptly renew their contract or find other housing for them.
