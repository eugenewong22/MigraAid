---
title: What housing must my employer provide?
source_ref: MOM — Housing standards for migrant workers
---
If your employer provides your accommodation, it must meet the housing standards set by the
authorities — including adequate space, ventilation, clean water, and safe cooking and toilet
facilities. Employers must declare where their workers live.

If your housing is overcrowded, unsafe, or unhygienic, you can report it to MOM or seek help from a
migrant worker organisation. You should not face punishment for raising a genuine concern about
your living conditions.
