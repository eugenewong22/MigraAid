Workers with relevant work experience in Singapore, a fixed monthly salary, academic qualifications or skills-based test certificates may be treated as higher-skilled workers. They may qualify for the higher-skilled worker levy.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Workers who possess relevant years of working experience in Singapore, fixed monthly salary, academic qualifications or skills-based test certificates may be considered as higher-skilled workers and qualify for higher-skilled worker levy .
