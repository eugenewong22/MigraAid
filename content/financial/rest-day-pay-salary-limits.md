You can claim pay for work on a rest day if you are a non-workman with a monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less. You can also claim it if you are a workman with a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> You can claim pay for work on a rest day if you are:
> 
> - A non-workman earning a monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less.
> - A workman earning a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less.
