You can claim overtime if you are a non-workman with monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less. You can also claim overtime if you are a workman with monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less. A workman generally does mainly manual labour.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> You can claim overtime if you are:
> 
> - A non-workman earning a monthly basic salary of $2,600 or less.
> - A workman earning a monthly basic salary of $4,500 or less. Generally, a workman is someone whose work involves mainly manual labour.
