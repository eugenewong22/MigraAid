---
title: What can my employer deduct from my salary?
source_ref: Employment Act — permitted deductions
---
Under the Employment Act, your employer can only make certain deductions from your salary, such as
for absence from work or with your written consent. Total deductions in a salary period generally
cannot exceed 50% of your salary for that period (some exceptions apply).

Employers cannot deduct money from your salary as a penalty, or to recover costs that are the
employer's responsibility. If you see deductions you do not understand, ask for an explanation and
check them against your itemised pay slip.
