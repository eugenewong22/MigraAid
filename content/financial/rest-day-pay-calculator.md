If you are covered by the Employment Act, you can use the calculator to work out pay for working on a rest day. A rest day is 1 whole day, from midnight to midnight. The rest day itself is not a paid day.

> If you are covered by the Employment Act , use this calculator to calculate your pay for working on a rest day. A rest day comprises 1 whole day (midnight to midnight). It is not a paid day.
