Medical leave wages are payable only for working days covered by MC, hospitalisation leave or light duty from a Singapore-registered doctor or dentist. They are not payable for non-working days, such as rest days and public holidays.

<!-- source-excerpt -->

> Medical leave wages are payable only for working days covered by MC, hospitalisation leave or light duty granted by Singapore-registered doctor or dentist. It is not payable for non-working days (e.g. rest days and public holidays).
